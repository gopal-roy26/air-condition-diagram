Air Conditioning System / Functional Flow

1 REFRIGERANT absorbs room heat in Evaporator.
2 COMPRESSOR pumps & compresses the gas.
3 Hot gas flows to OUTDOOR Condenser.
4 Heat is released outside & gas condenses to liquid.
5 EXPANSION VALVE cools liquid refrigerant rapidly.
6 Cycle repeats to continuously cool the air.
